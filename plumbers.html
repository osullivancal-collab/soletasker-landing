SoleTasker is a voice to task app for plumbers in Australia, designed for sole trader plumbers and small plumbing teams who work from the phone, the van and the job site.

A plumber moves from emergency calls to maintenance, quoting and return visits. Voice capture gives you a quick way to keep the facts attached to the right plumbing job while your hands are still on the tools.

Plumber voice-to-task FAQ

QUESTIONS PLUMBERS
ACTUALLY ASK.

What is a voice to task app for plumbers?

It lets a plumber speak a plumber job update and turn the useful parts into structured plumber tasks, reminders, job notes and follow-up instead of typing everything later.

Can a plumber capture invoice notes by voice?

Yes. A plumber can speak labour, materials and work completed into the plumber job record so the invoice detail is there when the admin is done.

Can I keep plumber photos and notes with the same job?

Yes. SoleTasker keeps plumber job notes, photos, work orders and follow-up together so the plumber record is useful when you return.

Is SoleTasker plumber job management software?

Yes, in a lightweight form. It is centred on plumber voice capture, plumber tasks and plumber job records rather than complex scheduling.

Can plumber tasks be assigned to a worker or admin person?

Yes. Plumber tasks can be assigned while staying linked to the relevant plumber job.

Can I save future plumbing work for later follow-up?

Yes. Future Work is useful for plumber opportunities such as a hot-water replacement, bathroom upgrade or preventative maintenance the customer is not ready to book yet.
