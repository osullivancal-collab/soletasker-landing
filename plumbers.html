SoleTasker is a voice to task app for plumbers in Australia, designed for sole trader plumbers and small plumbing teams who work from the phone, the van and the job site.

A plumber moves from emergency calls to maintenance, quoting and return visits. Voice capture gives you a quick way to keep the facts attached to the right plumbing job while your hands are still on the tools.

Capture the fault, repair, materials and any check-back before leaving the property.

Turn repeated call-outs into a clean record.

Use a short voice memo after each stop instead of relying on memory at the end of the day.

Plumber voice-to-task FAQ

QUESTIONS PLUMBERS
ACTUALLY ASK.

What is a voice to task app for plumbers?

It lets a plumber speak a job update and turn the useful parts into structured tasks, reminders, job notes and follow-up instead of typing everything later.

Can a plumber capture invoice notes by voice?

Yes. You can speak labour, materials and work completed into the job record so the invoice detail is there when the admin is done.

Can I keep plumber photos and notes with the same job?

Yes. SoleTasker keeps job notes, photos, work orders and follow-up together so the record is useful when you return.

Is SoleTasker plumber job management software?

Yes, in a lightweight form. It is centred on voice capture, tasks and job records rather than complex scheduling.

Can plumber tasks be assigned to a worker or admin person?

Yes. Tasks can be assigned while staying linked to the relevant plumbing job.

Can I save future plumbing work for later follow-up?

Yes. Future Work is useful for opportunities such as a hot-water replacement, bathroom upgrade or preventative maintenance the customer is not ready to book yet.
