SoleTasker is a voice to task app for HVAC technicians in Australia, designed for sole trader HVAC technicians and small HVAC teams working from the phone, van and site.

A service call often ends with a diagnosis, a part to order, a return visit and another system the customer wants looked at. Voice capture keeps those next steps with the job.

How voice-to-task works

SPEAK IT. REVIEW IT.
PUT IT WHERE IT BELONGS.

One voice memo can carry several different pieces of job admin. SoleTasker separates the useful actions so the job record stays useful instead of becoming one long transcript.

THE MOMENTS THAT CREATE
TOMORROW'S ADMIN.

The hard part is not remembering that you attended. It is remembering the unit, fault, part, follow-up and customer request once five more service calls have happened.

Service call

Keep the diagnosis and action together.

Capture what you found, what you did and whether anything needs ordering or monitoring.

Parts required

Create the order and return task immediately.

Do not leave the part requirement trapped in a photo of a compliance plate.

Commercial work order

Carry the work-order details into the job.

Keep site contact, order number, scope and service notes connected.

Future replacement

Keep the replacement opportunity without cluttering today.

Save the replacement conversation as Future Work instead of an immediate task.

HVAC technician voice-to-task FAQ

QUESTIONS HVAC TECHNICIANS
ACTUALLY ASK.

What is a voice to task app for HVAC technicians?

It lets an HVAC technician speak a service update and turn it into structured tasks, service notes, reminders and follow-up.

Can an HVAC technician capture service notes by voice?

Yes. You can speak the diagnosis, work completed, parts needed and follow-up while the service call is still fresh.

Can I track HVAC parts and return visits?

Yes. Create a parts task and a return visit or reminder linked to the same job.

Is SoleTasker HVAC job management software?

Yes, in a lightweight voice-first form for sole trader HVAC technicians and small HVAC teams.

Can I keep HVAC work orders and photos with the service job?

Yes. Work orders, photos and notes can stay with the job record.

Can I save HVAC replacement opportunities for later?

Yes. Future Work is designed for replacement systems or additional units that are not ready to proceed today.
