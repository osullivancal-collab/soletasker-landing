THE SOLAR INSTALL IS DONE.
CAPTURE WHAT'S STILL OPEN.

SoleTasker is a voice to task app for solar installers in Australia, designed for sole trader solar installers and small solar installation teams who work from the roof, switchboard, van and phone.

A solar installer can finish the physical install and still have admin, photos, a return item, monitoring setup or a battery conversation left behind. Voice capture keeps those details with the job.

How voice-to-task works

Solar installer voice-to-task FAQ

QUESTIONS SOLAR INSTALLERS
ACTUALLY ASK.

What is a voice to task app for solar installers?

It lets a solar installer speak a job update and turn it into structured tasks, site notes, reminders and follow-up.

Can a solar installer capture site notes and invoice notes by voice?

Yes. You can speak the work completed and invoice detail into the job record for later review.

Can I keep solar photos with the same job?

Yes. Photos can stay with the job alongside the installer notes and outstanding actions.

Can I track solar return work?

Yes. You can create a return task or reminder for monitoring setup, customer handover or another outstanding item.

Can solar installer tasks be assigned to another worker?

Yes. Tasks can be assigned while remaining linked to the relevant solar job.

Can I save battery and EV charger opportunities for later?

Yes. Future Work is designed for opportunities such as batteries, added capacity or related electrical work that is not ready to proceed today.
