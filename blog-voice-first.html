WHY WE BUILT IT THIS WAY

Why SoleTasker went voice-first.
Because tradies are already busy.

A new job can arrive by phone, in an email or as a work order. Getting those details into a system shouldn’t mean stopping to type everything out again.

By SoleTasker16 October 20265 minute read

Illustrative scenario; the image is AI-generated.

We built SoleTasker around a simple thing we kept seeing: tradies are expected to keep a lot of business information moving while they’re out doing the actual work.

A customer calls with a new job. A builder emails through a work order. You finish on site and remember the invoice needs a better description, a certificate still needs doing, or someone needs to come back next week. The details arrive in different places, but eventually someone has to get them into the system.

For a small trade business, that someone is often the same person who is quoting, driving, carrying tools and trying to keep the day on track. At the end of the job, retyping the scope, address, notes and next tasks can feel like another shift.

The admin starts before you reach a desk

Most tradies don’t spend the day sitting at a computer waiting to enter information. The customer may be on the phone while you’re between jobs. The work order might arrive by email while you’re on site. A useful detail can come to mind while you’re packing up or driving to the next job.

If capturing it takes too many steps, it’s easy to put it off until later. Then you’re trying to remember what was said, find the right email, or type the same information into more than one place. That’s where small but important details can get lost or delayed.

Voice is the quickest way to capture what just happened

That’s why we chose voice-first entry. When your hands are busy and the detail is fresh, you can say the next task or one detail you need to keep with the job. You don’t need to turn every thought into a long job summary.

SoleTasker turns spoken information into structured job notes and tasks. You can check the result and choose what to save. The point is to avoid starting with a blank form and manually typing every detail before you can move on.

Voice isn’t the only way in. You can upload job documents and send information to your unique SoleTasker intake URL by email. Use the method that suits what has arrived: speak a site update, attach the work order, or send through an email that belongs with the job.

Make the information useful to someone else

Getting the information into SoleTasker is only part of the job. It also needs to be clear enough to act on. “Certificate to do” or “send the photos to the builder” is a task someone can pick up when it’s connected to the right job and the details are there.

When someone helps with the business, a clear task and the right job details give them somewhere to start. They don’t have to piece the work order together from scattered emails or wait for you to explain what you meant.

Less retyping. More time back in your day.

We didn’t choose voice-first because every job should be handled by voice. We chose it because the work happens away from a desk, and the right information often arrives when stopping to type is the last thing you have time for.

Speak a task or short job detail. Upload a document. Send the email to your intake URL. Then keep the useful information with the job, where you or someone helping in the business can find it. It’s a small change in how work gets captured, with a practical aim: less admin to catch up on later, and more of your time left for the rest of your life.
