WALK THE SITE.
KEEP THE DETAILS WITH IT.

SoleTasker is a voice to task app for landscapers in Australia, designed for sole traders and small trade teams who work from the phone, the van and the job site.

Landscaping jobs mix design decisions, materials, weather, plant care and future maintenance. A voice-first job record makes it easier to capture what changed while you are physically looking at the site.

How voice-to-task works

SPEAK IT. REVIEW IT.
PUT IT WHERE IT BELONGS.

One voice memo can carry several different pieces of job admin. SoleTasker separates the useful actions so the job record stays useful instead of becoming one long transcript.

01

Say it while it is fresh

Capture the work completed, anything unfinished, admin notes and the next action before leaving the job.

02

Review the structured output

Check the wording before it becomes part of the job, a task, a reminder or future work.

03

Keep it with the job

Notes, photos, work orders and outstanding actions stay connected to the job they belong to.

Landscapers voice-to-task FAQ

QUESTIONS LANDSCAPERS
ACTUALLY ASK.

What is a voice to task app for landscapers?

It turns spoken landscaping job updates into structured tasks, site notes, reminders and follow-up.

Can I capture material lists by voice?

Yes. Speak the materials and quantities, review the result and save the action against the job.

Can I keep irrigation issues with the job?

Yes. Record the issue in the job and create an assigned task or return visit for the fix.

Is SoleTasker landscaping job management software?

Yes, but it is lightweight and voice-first rather than a heavy scheduling platform.

Can I save maintenance follow-ups?

Yes. Use reminders or Future Work for pruning, treatment, maintenance or a later stage the customer may want.

Can landscaping tasks be assigned to workers?

Yes. Tasks can be assigned to team members while remaining linked to the job context.
