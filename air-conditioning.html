SoleTasker is a voice to task app for air conditioning technicians in Australia, designed for sole traders and small trade teams who work from the phone, the van and the job site.

Air conditioning work often ends with a diagnosis, a part to order, a return visit and another system the customer wants looked at. Voice capture keeps those next steps together with the service job.

How voice-to-task works

SPEAK IT. REVIEW IT.
PUT IT WHERE IT BELONGS.

One voice memo can carry several different pieces of job admin. SoleTasker separates the useful actions so the job record stays useful instead of becoming one long transcript.

01

Say it while it is fresh

Capture the work completed, anything unfinished, admin notes and the next action before leaving the job.

02

Review the structured output

Check the wording before it becomes part of the job, a task, a reminder or future work.

03

Keep it with the job

Notes, photos, work orders and outstanding actions stay connected to the job they belong to.

THE MOMENTS THAT CREATE
TOMORROW'S ADMIN.

The tricky part is rarely remembering that you attended. It is remembering the model, fault, part, follow-up and customer request once five more service calls have happened.

Service call

Keep the diagnosis and action together.

Capture what you found, what you did and whether anything needs ordering or monitoring.

“Unit icing due to blocked filter. Cleaned today. Check performance next service and note the model in the job.”
Parts required

Create the order and return task immediately.

Do not rely on a photo of the compliance plate and a promise to remember it later.

“Daikin outdoor PCB fault. Save model details. Task: order PCB today. Return when stock arrives.”
Commercial work order

Carry the work-order details into the job.

Keep site contact, order number, scope and service notes connected.

“Work order 43182 complete. Rooftop unit two had belt replaced. Send notes to admin and invoice against the order.”
Future replacement

Keep the upgrade opportunity without cluttering today.

Save the replacement conversation as Future Work instead of turning it into an immediate task.

Air Conditioning voice-to-task FAQ

QUESTIONS AIR CONDITIONING
ACTUALLY ASK.

What is a voice to task app for air conditioning technicians?

It turns spoken service updates into structured tasks, job notes, reminders and follow-up for air conditioning and HVAC work.

Can I capture HVAC service notes by voice?

Yes. You can speak the diagnosis, work completed, parts needed and follow-up while the service call is still fresh.

Can I track parts and return visits?

Yes. Create a task for the part and a return visit or reminder linked to the same job.

Is SoleTasker HVAC job management software?

It is lightweight job management with a voice-first focus. It is intended for small operators rather than complex dispatch and scheduling.

Can I keep work orders and photos with the service job?

Yes. Work orders, photos and notes can stay with the job record.

Can I save replacement opportunities for later?

Yes. Future Work is designed for items such as replacement systems or additional units that are not ready to proceed today.
