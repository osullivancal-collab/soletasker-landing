SoleTasker is a voice to task app for builders in Australia, designed for sole traders and small trade teams who work from the phone, the van and the job site.

Builders collect information from clients, trades, drawings and site walks all day. A voice-first system is useful when the problem is not a lack of information — it is getting each piece into the right job before it disappears.

How voice-to-task works

SPEAK IT. REVIEW IT.
PUT IT WHERE IT BELONGS.

One voice memo can carry several different pieces of job admin. SoleTasker separates the useful actions so the job record stays useful instead of becoming one long transcript.

01

Say it while it is fresh

Capture the work completed, anything unfinished, admin notes and the next action before leaving the job.

02

Review the structured output

Check the wording before it becomes part of the job, a task, a reminder or future work.

03

Keep it with the job

Notes, photos, work orders and outstanding actions stay connected to the job they belong to.

THE MOMENTS THAT CREATE
TOMORROW'S ADMIN.

Defects, variations, trade follow-ups and client requests are usually spoken first. If they stay in calls, texts and memory, they turn into missed work and repeated conversations.

Site walk

Turn spoken defects into accountable tasks.

Capture room, issue, responsible trade and timing while you are standing in front of it.

“Bathroom one: silicone incomplete. Assign plumber. Bedroom two: cornice crack. Assign plasterer before paint starts.”
Client variation

Keep the request attached to the job.

Record what changed and create the follow-up without relying on a later text search.

“Client wants two extra pantry shelves. Save the note and remind me to price the variation tonight.”
Trade coordination

Capture the dependency, not just the message.

Turn a subcontractor call into a job-linked task with a clear next action.

DON'T JUST LOG A DEFECT.
LOG THE DEPENDENCY.

On a build, the valuable part is often who owns the issue and what has to happen before the next trade can move.

LocationRoom, level, elevation or area so nobody has to ask which item you meant.
IssueThe defect, incomplete work or client change in plain language.
Responsible tradeWho needs to own the next action.
DependencyWhat it is holding up — cabinetry, paint, fit-off, handover or another trade.
EvidenceThe photo, variation note or job note that gives the task enough context to close properly.

Builders voice-to-task FAQ

QUESTIONS BUILDERS
ACTUALLY ASK.

What is a voice to task app for builders?

It lets a builder speak site updates and turn them into structured tasks, job notes, reminders and follow-up while walking the job.

Can SoleTasker be used for defect lists?

Yes. You can capture defect items as tasks and keep them linked to the relevant job.

Can I record variations by voice?

You can record the variation detail as a job note and create a pricing or follow-up task. SoleTasker does not replace your contract or variation approval process.

Is SoleTasker builder job management software?

Yes, but it is deliberately lightweight and voice-first rather than a full construction scheduling platform.

Can I assign builder tasks to workers or admin?

Yes. Tasks can be assigned to people on the team and remain attached to the job context.

Can I keep photos and work orders with the job?

Yes. Photos, plans, work orders and notes can sit alongside the job record and its outstanding actions.
