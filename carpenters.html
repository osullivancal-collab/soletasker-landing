Carpenters job management, without the desk work

MEASURE IT.
DON'T TYPE IT TWICE.

SoleTasker is a voice to task app for carpenters in Australia, designed for sole traders and small trade teams who work from the phone, the van and the job site.

Carpentry is full of measurements, material lists, client changes and little return items. Voice capture is useful when you want those details attached to the job before you get back in the van.

How voice-to-task works

SPEAK IT. REVIEW IT.
PUT IT WHERE IT BELONGS.

One voice memo can carry several different pieces of job admin. SoleTasker separates the useful actions so the job record stays useful instead of becoming one long transcript.

THE MOMENTS THAT CREATE
TOMORROW'S ADMIN.

A number in a text thread, a timber list in Notes and a client change remembered from site can all be correct — and still be impossible to find when you need them.

Measure-up

Keep dimensions with the job.

Speak key measurements and what they relate to instead of leaving them in a generic note.

“Laundry opening 1830 wide, 2120 high. Save under cabinetry prep and attach today's photos.”
Materials

Turn the missing material into a task.

Capture what is needed and when you need it before leaving the supplier run to memory.

“Need four lengths 70 by 35 treated pine and two boxes of 75mm screws. Pick up tomorrow morning.”
Client change

Record the change and the next action.

Save the request in the job and create a follow-up if pricing or confirmation is still required.

“Client wants the bench extended 300mm. Save as site note and task me to price it tonight.”
Return item

Keep unfinished carpentry visible.

Record the last small item so it does not disappear once the main work looks finished.

“Wardrobe complete except two handles on backorder. Return and fit when supplier texts.”

Carpenters voice-to-task FAQ

QUESTIONS CARPENTERS
ACTUALLY ASK.

What is a voice to task app for carpenters?

It lets a carpenter speak job details and turn them into structured tasks, notes, reminders and follow-up instead of typing them later.

Can I save measurements in a carpenter job record?

Yes. Measurements can be captured in job or site notes so they remain with the relevant work.

Can I create material pickup tasks by voice?

Yes. Speak the material and due timing, review it, and save it as a task.

Is SoleTasker carpenter job management software?

Yes, in a lightweight voice-first form for sole traders and small teams.

Can I record client changes against the job?

Yes. Save the change as a job note and create any pricing or confirmation task that follows from it.

Can I keep photos and plans with the carpentry job?

Yes. Photos and plans can be stored with the job alongside notes and outstanding actions.
