Electrician use cases

FROM SERVICE CALL TO JOB WRAP-UP.

The useful part is not “AI for electricians”. It is having one quick way to get real electrical job information out of your head and into a usable job record.

Fault finding · leaving site

You found the fault, made it safe and need to come back with the right part.

“Fault traced to damaged contactor in pool plant. Isolated and left safe. Task for me: order replacement contactor tomorrow. Return Friday. Add two hours fault-finding to invoice notes.”

SoleTasker keeps the site note, return task and invoice detail together instead of leaving them in three different places.
Switchboard upgrade · complete

The physical work is finished, but the paperwork still has to follow.

“Switchboard upgrade complete. New RCBOs, main earth upgraded and board labelled. Save that for the invoice and certificate. Add board photos. Cert to do Monday.”

The job record carries the work description, certificate note, photos and follow-up task forward.
Customer asks about later work

You are packing up and the customer mentions an EV charger they might do next year.

“Future work: customer interested in EV charger after they replace the car. Follow up in February. Existing board may need assessment first.”

Future work stays attached to the customer and job instead of living in your memory.
Small electrical team

Your worker calls with something that needs action, but you still need it on your radar.

“Assign Tom: replace damaged isolator at Richmond tomorrow. Add it to My Day as well so I can check it is closed out.”

Assign the task without losing your own visibility of what still needs attention.

Electrician voice-to-task FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS.

What is a voice-to-task app for electricians?
A voice-to-task app lets an electrician speak a job update naturally and turn it into structured work such as tasks, reminders, site notes and job follow-up. SoleTasker is built around this voice-first workflow for Australian tradies.
Can SoleTasker turn electrician voice notes into job notes and tasks?
Yes. You can speak naturally and review the result before saving it as a task, reminder, job note or other supported job action.
Can I capture invoice and electrical certificate notes by voice?
Yes. SoleTasker can keep invoice notes and certificate descriptions with the job so the wording is there when you or your admin are ready to finish the paperwork.
Does SoleTasker work with Xero?
Yes. SoleTasker can push a draft invoice to Xero from the job workflow, while keeping the job notes and invoice detail together in SoleTasker.
Is SoleTasker suitable for sole trader electricians and small electrical teams?
Yes. SoleTasker is designed for sole traders and small trade teams that want lightweight voice-first job admin without turning the day into desk work.
