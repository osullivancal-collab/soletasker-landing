THE MOMENTS THAT CREATE
TOMORROW'S ADMIN.

Electrical jobs rarely finish with one neat outcome. There is usually a cert to do, something for the invoice, a return visit, a customer request or another job worth following up.

End of a call-out

Turn the job wrap-up into the admin list.

Save what was done, what needs invoicing, certificate wording and any unfinished work in one pass.

“Fault found at the hot-water circuit. Replaced burnt isolator. Certificate required. Invoice two hours plus isolator. Follow up next month about the old switchboard.”
Between jobs

Turn a phone call into a task before it disappears.

Capture the customer, job context, priority and next action without pulling over to type a full note.

“Call Mark at Brighton after 4. Power keeps tripping when the oven is on. Make it a P1 task for this afternoon.”
Return visits

Keep rough-in, fit-off and return work connected.

Record what is finished now and what still needs another visit so the job history makes sense when you come back.

“Kitchen rough-in done. Still waiting on pendants and rangehood. Return once cabinetry is finished.”
Small team

Assign the follow-up without sending another message later.

Create a task for your worker or admin person while keeping it linked to the relevant electrical job.

“Laura to finish the invoice and certificate. Tom to pick up the RCBO before Friday's return visit.”
Electrician close-out checklist

ONE WRAP-UP.
FIVE DIFFERENT PLACES.

An electrical job can be physically finished while the admin is not. This is the information worth capturing before you leave the driveway.

Site noteWhat was actually installed, repaired, tested or found on site.
Invoice notesLabour, materials, extras and the useful description your invoice needs later.
Certificate notesThe work description you will need when the electrical certificate is completed.
Return itemAnything unfinished, on backorder or needing another visit.
Future workThe switchboard, EV charger or lighting upgrade the customer mentioned but is not ready for yet.

That is why SoleTasker treats an electrician wrap-up as several reviewed outputs, not one giant transcript.

Electricians voice-to-task FAQ

QUESTIONS ELECTRICIANS
ACTUALLY ASK.

What is a voice to task app for electricians?

A voice to task app lets an electrician speak a job update instead of typing it, then turns the useful parts into structured actions such as tasks, reminders, site notes or job admin. SoleTasker is built around that workflow for Australian tradies.

Can SoleTasker turn electrician voice notes into job notes and tasks?

Yes. A voice memo can be reviewed and separated into items such as a site note, task, reminder, unfinished item, future work or other job-linked admin instead of being saved only as a block of transcript text.

Can I capture invoice and electrical certificate notes by voice?

Yes. You can speak the invoice detail and certificate work description as part of a job wrap-up so the wording is kept with the job for later review and completion.

Is SoleTasker electrician job management software?

Yes, but it is intentionally lightweight and voice-first. It keeps jobs, tasks, notes, photos, work orders, follow-up and admin outputs together without making a large scheduling system the centre of the product.

Does it work for sole trader electricians and small electrical teams?

Yes. SoleTasker is designed for sole traders and small teams. Tasks can be assigned to a worker or admin person while the owner keeps the job context and follow-up visible.

Can I use SoleTasker for return visits and unfinished electrical work?

Yes. You can record unfinished items, create a return task or visit, and keep the detail linked to the same job so rough-in, fit-off and follow-up work are easier to pick up later.
