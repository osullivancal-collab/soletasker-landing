Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

SoleTasker ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (soletasker.com.au) and use our voice-to-task management platform.

We comply with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Privacy (Australian Government Agencies – Management) Directions 2024.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you sign up for SoleTasker, we collect:

  • Email address — for account creation and communication
  • Mobile number (optional) — to support two-factor authentication and account recovery
  • Trade type — to personalize your experience (e.g., Electrician, Plumber, Builder)
  • Payment information — credit/debit card details processed securely by Stripe

2.2 Business Data

When you use SoleTasker, we collect and store:

  • Voice recordings and transcripts — your spoken job notes, tasks, and reminders
  • Job notes and task data — structured information created from your voice input
  • Photos and attachments — before/after photos, certificates, and job documentation
  • Client information — names, contact details, job details shared with clients
  • Invoice and payment data — records of work completed and money owed
  • Usage logs — timestamps, features used, device information

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address and browser type
  • Device information and operating system
  • Referring URLs and pages visited
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for:

  • Service delivery — providing, maintaining, and improving SoleTasker
  • Payment processing — charging subscription fees via Stripe
  • Communication — sending you service updates, billing notifications, and support responses
  • Authentication — verifying your identity and securing your account
  • Analytics — understanding how you use SoleTasker to make it better
  • Legal compliance — meeting Australian tax, employment, and consumer protection laws
  • Fraud prevention — detecting and preventing fraudulent transactions

4. Payment Information and Stripe

SoleTasker uses Stripe to process all payments securely. We do NOT store your full credit card details on our servers. Here's how it works:

  • Your card information is encrypted and transmitted directly to Stripe's secure servers
  • We only store a tokenized reference to process future charges
  • All transactions comply with PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)
  • For details about Stripe's privacy practices, see: stripe.com/privacy

5. Data Storage and Security

Your business data is stored on secure, encrypted servers in Australia-based data centers. We implement:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Restricted access — only authorized staff can access your data
  • Automatic backups with geographic redundancy

6. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may disclose it to:

  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • Third-party service providers — hosting, analytics, email (Australian data centers only)
  • Law enforcement — when required by Australian law or court order
  • Your clients — only job information you choose to share via job intake links

We do not transfer personal information outside Australia without your explicit consent or legal requirement.

7. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction — request we correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and data (except where legally required to retain)
  • Portability — export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@soletasker.com.au

8. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Your data is retained while your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: Personal data is deleted within 30 days; business data may be retained for 7 years to comply with Australian tax law
  • Backups: Deleted data may remain in backups for up to 90 days for disaster recovery

9. Cookie Policy

SoleTasker uses cookies to:

  • Keep you logged in securely
  • Remember your preferences
  • Track website performance and usage
  • Prevent fraud

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

10. Third-Party Links

SoleTasker may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., Stripe, external documentation). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Always review their privacy policies before providing information.

11. Children's Privacy

SoleTasker is not directed to users under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we learn we've collected data from a minor, we will delete it immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. We'll notify you of material changes via email or by posting the updated policy on our website. Your continued use of SoleTasker after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@soletasker.com.au
  • Address: Australia

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au
