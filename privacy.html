Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

SoleTasker ("we", "us" or "our") provides a voice-first job and admin service. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit soletasker.com.au or use SoleTasker. We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable Australian privacy laws.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use SoleTasker, information may include:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, business or trade information and account preferences.
  • Job and customer information you enter or receive, including names, contact details, addresses, job details, notes, tasks and reminders.
  • Voice and transcription data when you use voice-capture features.
  • Documents and media such as job photos, plans, work orders, certificates and other files you choose to upload.
  • Team information you provide when using assignment or team features.
  • Subscription information such as subscription status and payment-related identifiers. Full card details are processed by Stripe rather than stored by SoleTasker.
  • Technical and usage information that may include IP address, browser/device information, timestamps, diagnostic information and feature usage where generated by our systems or service providers.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide, secure and maintain SoleTasker.
  • Authenticate users and keep accounts separated.
  • Transcribe voice and process information needed for SoleTasker features.
  • Store and display jobs, tasks, reminders, files and related business records.
  • Process subscriptions and manage billing.
  • Provide customer support and service communications.
  • Detect abuse, diagnose failures and improve reliability and usability.
  • Meet legal obligations and protect our users, service and rights.

4. Payments

SoleTasker uses Stripe to process subscription payments. Card information entered during checkout is handled by Stripe. SoleTasker may receive and store payment-related identifiers and subscription information, such as a Stripe customer identifier, subscription status and transaction-related information needed to operate billing. We do not store your full card number.

5. Service Providers and Overseas Processing

SoleTasker relies on third-party service providers to operate the service. These may provide hosting, database, authentication, file storage, transcription or AI processing, email, analytics/diagnostics and payment processing. Information may be processed or stored in countries outside Australia depending on the provider and service configuration. We take reasonable steps appropriate to our circumstances when selecting and configuring providers and handling personal information.

Our providers may change as SoleTasker develops. We do not sell customer personal information.

6. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. These include access controls and security features provided by our infrastructure providers, and encryption in transit and at rest where supported by the relevant service. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Customer and Third-Party Information

If you enter personal information about customers, workers or other people, you are responsible for ensuring you are permitted to collect and use that information for your business purposes. SoleTasker processes that information to provide the service to you.

8. Access, Correction and Deletion Requests

You may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you, or to request deletion of your account and associated information. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and our technical and legal obligations. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate security or dispute purposes, or for a limited period in backups.

Requests can be sent to privacy@soletasker.com.au.

9. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SoleTasker, maintain security, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods vary according to the type of information. When information is no longer reasonably required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it where appropriate. Backup copies may persist for a limited period as part of normal disaster-recovery processes.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

SoleTasker and its service providers may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies where needed for authentication, security, preferences, performance and understanding use of the website or app. Browser controls may allow you to restrict these technologies, although doing so can affect functionality.

11. Children

SoleTasker is intended for business users aged 18 or over.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy as SoleTasker, our providers or legal obligations change. The current version will be published with its last-updated date.

13. Contact and Complaints

Privacy questions or complaints can be sent to privacy@soletasker.com.au. We will take reasonable steps to investigate privacy complaints. Where applicable, you may also have the right to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
