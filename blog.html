A SOLE TRADER STORY

The admin isn’t the problem.
It’s that everything is stuck in your head.

For a sole-trader electrician, the workday can end at the front door. The calls, quotes, certificates and things to do often don’t.

By SoleTasker14 October 20266 minute read

Illustrative scenario, not a customer testimonial.

The kids are doing homework. Your partner has opened the laptop because she wants to take a few things off your plate. You’re trying to remember whether the builder’s photos were sent, whether the certificate is done and what you promised the customer you’d price tomorrow.

None of those jobs is enormous on its own. The hard part is carrying all of them at once — across calls, texts, emails and the notes you meant to write before leaving site. You want the help, and she wants to give it, but the details are buried in your inbox or still only in your head. Before she can pick up a task, you have to stop and explain the whole job.

When every task lives in your head

A small electrical business can produce a surprising amount of unfinished admin: a client to call back, materials to order, an invoice description to prepare, a certificate to complete and a builder waiting on photos. If each one relies on you remembering it later, they all compete for attention at the same time.

That mental load can spill into home life. Your partner may be ready to send the photos, prepare the invoice or keep a customer’s quote moving. But if the email is hard to find and the rest of the context is with you, helping can turn into another round of questions. Sometimes it feels quicker to keep the task yourself, even when you’re both tired of that arrangement.

Get the next action out of your head

The useful moment to record something is often the moment you remember it: while you’re in the van, between jobs or finishing up on site. A quick voice note can capture what happened and what needs to happen next. A voice-to-task tool for tradies should make that capture quick, then leave you in control of where the details go.

With SoleTasker, you can speak a task, reminder or job wrap-up, review what’s been captured, then save it against the right job. For example: “Smith Street is finished. Invoice for $1,400 plus GST. Certificate to do. Email the builder the photos. Check the EV charger quote next month.”

Give your wife a clear way to help

Once you capture the job details, you can turn the next steps into tasks and assign them to your wife: prepare the invoice, send the builder the photos, or get the certificate ready. She can see what needs doing and the job details you’ve saved, without piecing it together from your emails or stopping you to explain it all.

That gives her a real way to be part of the business and takes some of the admin off your shoulders. Instead of carrying every task yourself, you can share the work, keep jobs moving, and be more present with your kids and the rest of your life.

Less business admin at the kitchen table

SoleTasker isn’t a promise that paperwork disappears, and it doesn’t replace your invoicing software. It gives you a simple voice-to-task way to capture the work, keep job notes and documents together, and hand off tasks when someone else can help.

For a sole trader, that small change matters. The details are somewhere you can both find them. Your partner can see what needs doing and has enough context to get started without pulling you back into work. And when you’re home, you have a better chance of being present instead of mentally replaying the day.
