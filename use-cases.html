THE JOB HAPPENS FIRST.
SOLE TASKER FITS AROUND IT.

You do not need to stop work and “do admin”. Capture the detail when it comes up, keep it with the job, and come back to it when it actually needs attention.

01 · New enquiry while you are already working

SEND THE INTAKE LINK.
LET THEM FILL IN THE BASICS.

A customer calls while you are halfway through another job. Instead of stopping to type out the address, contact details and job description, send your SoleTasker intake link. They add the details and photos; you review it when you are ready.

Example

You are on a switchboard upgrade. A new customer wants a split-system quote. Send the link, finish the job in front of you, then review the enquiry later.

02 · Customer mentions work for later

KEEP THE “MAYBE LATER” WORK
WITH THE JOB.

The customer points at the garage and asks about an EV charger, or mentions they may want the switchboard upgraded next month. It is not today’s task and it does not need a quote right now — but it is worth remembering.

Example

Tag “EV charger + switchboard upgrade” as Future Work against the customer’s job. When you are ready to follow up, the context is still there.

●

“Customer asked about an EV charger and switchboard upgrade. Follow up after handover.”

04 · You have a worker, partner or admin helping

ASSIGN THE TASK.
KEEP YOUR OWN EYE ON IT.

A task can belong to someone else without disappearing from your radar. Assign the work to the right person, and if you want to keep watching it, add that same task to your own My Day. Assignment and My Day are two different things.

Example

Tom is assigned to collect the RCBOs. You still add that task to your own My Day because the afternoon job depends on it.

05 · Next morning

OPEN MY DAY.
SEE WHAT YOU CHOSE FOR TODAY.

My Day is not a dispatch board and it is not another copy of Tasks. It is the short list you deliberately bring into today. Filter to yourself, one worker or everyone, then work through it without changing the underlying task or assignee.

Example

Your own list has the overdue quote, today’s certificate and Tom’s RCBO pickup because you want to keep an eye on all three.

06 · A work order or PDF lands in front of you

SCAN THE DOCUMENT.
CHECK THE DETAILS. SAVE IT.

When a work order arrives as paper, photo or PDF, Doc Scan helps pull out the useful job information so you do not have to retype it. You still review the result before saving it.

Example

A real-estate work order arrives with the address, tenant contact and order number. Scan it, check those details, then create or update the job.
