Straight answers

Questions before
you try it.

Is SoleTasker another ServiceM8?

No. SoleTasker is intentionally lighter. It helps you capture jobs, assign tasks and remember follow-up without asking you to run your whole business through a large field-service system.

Can I give a task to someone on my team?

Yes. You can assign a task to a team member, including while capturing it by voice, so it is clear who owns the next step.

What can I say in a voice memo?

A new job, a task, a reminder, a customer follow-up or a future prospect. Each voice memo can be up to two minutes.

Does it replace my accounting software?

No. SoleTasker is focused on the practical work around jobs and admin. It is not trying to replace specialist accounting software.

Is the price really $24 a month?

That is the planned launch price in AUD, including GST. Early-access members will be contacted before any billing begins.
