How it works

Speak it once. Save it properly.

No typing. No forms to hunt through mid-job. Say what needs doing — SoleTasker transcribes it and reads out the job it belongs to, the priority and who it's for.

1

Open Home

Tap Voice Capture to start recording — no menus to dig through first.

2

Speak it once

Tell it what you need — a job, a task, a reminder. Two minutes max, then it's transcribed and checked for priority, job and assignee.

3

Review & save

It lands as the right task, with details and priority filled in — you just check it and save.

Ask for the review

Opens a message already written, with your Google review link in it. Send it by SMS or email while the job’s fresh.

Write down the extra work

Anything you spot but can’t do today goes on the job’s Unfinished Items list — not a scrap of paper in the van.
